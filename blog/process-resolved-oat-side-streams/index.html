Research note

Process-resolved oat side streams for food use

20 August 2026

A research note on turning processing side streams into useful food ingredients through better characterisation and process control.

Cereal processing can generate streams with very different composition and functionality depending on where they originate in the process. Treating all side streams as one material can hide useful differences.

A process-resolved approach links each fraction to its origin, composition and potential application. This can support more targeted strategies for food use and value creation.
