Academic publications are essential, but they are not always the best format for explaining the thinking around a research question.

This section of the website is a place for shorter notes: useful observations from projects, reflections after conferences, explanations of analytical methods, and accessible discussions of food processing and ingredient science.

The goal is not to publish results prematurely. It is to make the context around the research easier to understand and to create a durable record of ideas that may be useful to collaborators, students and others working in food science.

I expect most posts to stay concise and practical. Where a topic relates to published work, I will link back to the formal paper or scholarly record.
