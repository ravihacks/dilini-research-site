<p class="eyebrow">Research</p>
<h1>Understanding how processing changes food.</h1>
<p class="lede">My research connects processing conditions with structure, functionality, nutritional behaviour and product quality.</p>
</section>

<section class="section">
<div class="container research-list">
<article><span>01</span><div><h2>Grains and oats</h2><p>I study how grain composition and processing influence functional quality, ingredient behaviour and opportunities for new food applications.</p></div></article>
<article><span>02</span><div><h2>Plant proteins and ingredients</h2><p>I am interested in plant-derived ingredients and the relationships between composition, processing, functionality and nutritional quality.</p></div></article>
<article><span>03</span><div><h2>Thermal and non-thermal processing</h2><p>Processing can alter structure, viscosity, digestibility, flavour and stability. I investigate these changes to support evidence-based process design.</p></div></article>
<article><span>04</span><div><h2>Digestibility and shelf-life</h2><p>I examine how food structure and processing influence digestion kinetics, quality retention and storage performance.</p></div></article>
<article><span>05</span><div><h2>Side-stream utilisation</h2><p>I am interested in creating higher-value food uses for processing side streams and underutilised fractions where the science supports practical application.</p></div></article>
</div>
</section>
