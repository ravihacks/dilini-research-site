<h2>Background</h2>
</div>
<div class="prose">
<p>My research spans grains, oats, plant proteins, food processing, digestibility and shelf-life. I enjoy working across fundamental analysis and applied product questions, connecting laboratory evidence with practical food-system outcomes.</p>
<p>I currently work as a Senior Research Associate at the Queensland Alliance for Agriculture and Food Innovation (QAAFI), The University of Queensland.</p>
<p>This website brings together my research interests, selected projects, publications and short research notes in one place.</p>
