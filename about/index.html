About

Food science focused on useful, real-world outcomes.

I am a food scientist and researcher interested in how processing influences food structure, ingredient functionality, nutrition and product quality.

Background

My research spans grains, oats, plant proteins, food processing, digestibility and shelf-life. I enjoy working across fundamental analysis and applied product questions, connecting laboratory evidence with practical food-system outcomes.

I currently work as a Senior Research Associate at the Queensland Alliance for Agriculture and Food Innovation (QAAFI), The University of Queensland.

This website brings together my research interests, selected projects, publications and short research notes.
